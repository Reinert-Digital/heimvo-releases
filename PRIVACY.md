## The Heimvo software

Heimvo runs on your own device. Data about devices, connections and alerts is stored there and **not sent to us**. Heimvo contains no telemetry and no account with us; the license check runs offline.

Connections to third parties:

- **On by default:** clock check against a public time server (`pool.ntp.org`), which receives your IP address. On the Heimvo box, Heimvo also checks GitHub for updates and downloads them from there. Both can be switched off.
- **Only if you switch them on or set them up:** notifications (email, Telegram, Teams, Slack, ntfy, webhook), lists of known malicious addresses (abuse.ch), GeoIP data (db-ip.com), update check on Windows. The privacy notices of those providers apply.

Whoever runs Heimvo in a network is responsible for that use, not us. Notes for practices, law firms and offices are in the [FAQ](README.en.md#faq).

## Requests and free Pro keys

If you write to us, for example for a free Pro key, we process your name or company name, your email address and the content of your message.

- **Purpose:** issuing and sending the license key and answering your questions about Heimvo. We use these details only for that.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the license at your request); for other questions Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering requests).
- **In the key:** the license key permanently contains the name or company it was issued to and the issue and expiry dates, no email address. It is stored only on your device.
- **Recipients (processors):** ALL-INKL.COM, Friedersdorf, Germany (mailbox); our system Onyx on servers of 24fire GmbH, Schwetzingen, Germany, with backups at Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany, behind Cloudflare, Inc. (USA; transfer based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).
- **Retention:** name or company and expiry date of an issued key until the key expires plus three months; email copies in Onyx for two years.
- **Marketing:** you receive information about other offers only if you expressly consented (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time.
